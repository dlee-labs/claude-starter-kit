# Members' AI privacy notice (template)

A short page for your church website, linked from the footer and the privacy page. It tells members, in plain
words, what the policy in `ai-use-policy.md` means for them. Replace every `[BRACKETED]` value, and change any
line your leaders changed in the policy: the notice must never promise more than the policy does.

**Before publishing:** confirm your meeting tool can hold the deletion setting named below.

---

## How we use AI at [CHURCH NAME]

Our staff use AI tools on the church's business account for the day-to-day work of running a church: planning
events, building schedules, drafting emails, preparing Sunday slides, and carrying each sermon into the week.
Here is what that means for your information.

**AI sees only what the staff member already could.** When a staff member uses AI with our church database, it
works through their own login. If they can't open your record, the AI working for them can't either.

**Your information is not used to train AI.** Our AI services are bound by contract not to train on what we give
them.

**Some things have their own protection.**

- Your giving is never used in shepherding or ministry decisions. Only the few people who handle the church's
  finances see individual gifts.
- What you share in counseling stays within counseling and the pastoral oversight your counseling agreement
  describes. Our counselors use AI to help with their notes only if you give written permission, asked for
  plainly on the intake form, and you can withdraw it at any time.
- We never put church information about people into free or personal AI accounts.
- AI never responds to someone in crisis or grief. A person does.
- If we use AI to help leaders notice who might welcome a call, it counts only what we already record, like group
  attendance and serving. It never judges anyone's spiritual state.

**Words sent to you are a person's words.** A staff member may use AI to help draft a message, but they read it,
own it, and stand behind it before it goes out. When a message is personal or pastoral, its heart comes from the
person, not from AI. Our pastors write their sermons.

**Meetings may be recorded.** Before a meeting is recorded, everyone in it is asked, including anyone who joins
late, and saying no is always fine; you can tell the organizer privately. If you change your mind later, ask and
we will delete the recording. Recordings and transcripts are stored securely in the church's account, never made
public, and deleted after [one month]. A summary is kept, the way meeting minutes always have been, and summaries
of pastoral discussion are kept privately with the [elders'] records. Counseling sessions are not recorded this
way.

**We don't fake real moments.** We won't show you an AI image and call it a photo of something that happened at
[CHURCH NAME].

We review these practices as the technology changes, and [the elders] may pause or stop any use as we learn.

If you have a question about any of this, ask a pastor or an elder, or write to us at [CONTACT]. We're glad to
tell you exactly what we do.
